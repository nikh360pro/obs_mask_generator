How to Use a Virtual Background Without a Green Screen

Traditional virtual backgrounds require a physical green screen for accurate edge detection. This free tool uses AI to cut out your body from the webcam feed in real time, replacing your background with any color — entirely in your browser.

  1. Click Start Webcam and allow camera access when prompted.
  2. Choose a background style: blur, chroma green, black, or purple.
  3. Adjust Edge Smoothing to improve the cut-out quality around your edges.
  4. For Zoom or Teams: enable OBS Virtual Camera and select it as your webcam source.
  5. For OBS streaming: add this page as a Browser Source.

What is Chroma Key Green and Why Use It?

Chroma key green is the exact shade used by professional TV studios to remove backgrounds. When you select the Chroma Green background in this tool, OBS Studio can key it out perfectly using its built-in Chroma Key filter — giving you ultra-clean edges that even beat physical green screens for indoor use.

Does the Quality Match a Real Green Screen?

For most indoor conditions under good lighting — yes. Our AI segmentation model produces edges that rival a physical green screen at 15-30 FPS on most modern laptops using WebGL acceleration. The key advantage is that you need no physical setup, no special lighting, and no dedicated camera.
