About OBS Mask Generator

We build free, high-performance browser tools for streamers. No downloads, no signups, just instant results.

Our Story

Hi, I'm Nikhil Agarwal, the creator of OBS Mask Generator. I actively streamed from 2019 to 2022, and during that time, I noticed a recurring problem: many streamers were struggling to simply shape their facecam. Not everyone has Photoshop or the technical skills to create custom transparent overlays.

I decided to build a suite of completely free, browser-based solutions to make streamers' lives easier. Today, this site serves thousands of creators daily, providing them with the essential utilities they need to make their broadcasts look professional without the hassle.

Why Trust Us?

Unlike SEO-farmed tool sites, these tools were built by someone who actually streamed for years. I understand the nuances of bitrate limits, the frustration of OBS audio sync issues, and the importance of CPU-efficient browser sources.

  • ✅ Privacy First: All image processing happens locally in your browser. Your files never touch our servers.
  • ✅ Performance Focused: Our tools are lightweight and won't lag your stream.
  • ✅ Built for Creators: Designed specifically to solve real problems faced by streamers.

Meet the Creator

Nikhil Agarwal is a developer and former Twitch streamer (2019–2022) based in Greater Noida, India. After years of dealing with clunky, paid tools for basic streaming tasks, he built OBS Mask Generator to give creators something genuinely free and fast.

He now maintains and grows this suite of 20+ free streaming tools, focusing on browser-based, privacy-first utilities that work without logins or downloads.
