About OBS Mask Generator

OBS Mask Generator is a free, browser-based suite of 20+ tools for streamers and content creators. No downloads, no accounts, no paywalls — just instant results. Every tool runs entirely in your browser.

Our Story

Hi, I'm Nikhil Agarwal, the developer behind OBS Mask Generator. I actively streamed on Twitch from 2019 to 2022, and during that time I ran into the same frustrating problem over and over: the tools streamers need are either locked behind expensive subscriptions, require complicated software installations, or simply don't exist.

The specific problem that started everything: I wanted a circular webcam in OBS. Every guide online pointed to Photoshop or expensive overlay packs. I thought — this should take 10 seconds, not 10 minutes. So I built a simple mask generator that weekend. That tool got shared in a few Discord servers, and within weeks thousands of streamers were using it.

I realised there was a massive gap in the market for fast, free, privacy-first tools built specifically for content creators. I've been building ever since.

Our Philosophy

The streaming industry is increasingly full of tools that are "free to try" but hit you with a paywall for basic features. I've been that frustrated creator who just needed to resize an emote at 11 PM before going live and couldn't because of a subscription gate.

This site exists to fix that. Every tool here is free — not a trial, not a limited preview, not "free with watermark." Genuinely, completely free. The site is supported by advertising, which keeps the lights on without charging creators anything.
