Nikhil Agarwal

Streaming Tools Developer & Former Twitch Streamer

I streamed on Twitch from 2019 to 2022. During those years, I ran into the same problem every smaller creator faces: the tools that make your stream look professional are either expensive, require a subscription, or are just badly designed.

So I built my own. OBS Mask Generator started as a single tool to create webcam masks without Photoshop. It's now a suite of 20+ free, browser-based tools used by thousands of streamers daily — from Twitch affiliates to YouTube creators.

I'm based in Greater Noida, India. I focus on building fast, privacy-first tools that work without accounts, without logins, and without hidden costs.
