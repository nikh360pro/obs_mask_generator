How to Blur Your Webcam Background Online

This free tool uses AI to detect you in real time and apply a professional background blur directly in your browser. No Zoom subscription, no Teams premium plan, no software download required.

  1. Click Start Webcam and allow camera access when prompted.
  2. Your background will be blurred automatically within seconds.
  3. Adjust the Blur Strength slider to control how intense the blur is.
  4. Use Edge Smoothing to clean the border between you and your background.
  5. To use with OBS, add this page as a Browser Source in OBS Studio.

Does This Work Without a Green Screen?

Yes, completely. This tool uses an AI segmentation model that runs in your browser using WebGL acceleration. It detects the outline of your body and face in real time, then applies the blur only to what is behind you. No green screen, no special lighting, and no expensive camera is required.

Which Apps Can I Use This With?

  • Zoom: Use OBS Virtual Camera as your video source in Zoom settings.
  • Microsoft Teams: Set OBS Virtual Camera as your webcam input in Teams.
  • Google Meet: Use OBS Virtual Camera as a custom webcam source.
  • OBS Studio: Add this page directly as a Browser Source.
  • Twitch and YouTube Live: Works through OBS Browser Source natively.

Zoom and Teams background blur requires a paid subscription or specific hardware. This free tool works on any computer made after 2016, runs entirely in your browser, and works with any video conferencing app through OBS Virtual Camera. You get the same professional look at zero cost.
