ShadowPlay Not Recording? How to Fix NVIDIA Instant Replay

By Nikhil Agarwal • Published July 2026 • 5 min read

NVIDIA ShadowPlay (now part of the NVIDIA App/GeForce Experience) is incredible for clipping epic moments with zero performance loss. But there is a massive catch: it is notorious for silently turning itself off, leaving you frantically mashing your hotkey with nothing to show for it. Here is how to fix it immediately.

🚀 The Quick Answer

If ShadowPlay refuses to turn on or record, the most common fix is to turn on "Desktop Capture" in your Privacy Control settings, or to simply restart the NVIDIA Local System Container service in Windows Services.

Why is NVIDIA ShadowPlay Not Recording? (5 Quick Fixes)

As a heavy user of Instant Replay, I've run into every single ShadowPlay bug imaginable. Usually, the issue stems from Windows permissions, a hanging background service, or a misconfigured audio track. Let's run through the definitive checklist to get your clips saving again.

1. Turn on Privacy Control (Desktop Capture)

If you are playing a game in "Borderless Windowed" mode, or playing a game that NVIDIA doesn't officially recognize, ShadowPlay won't record it because it thinks you are just staring at your desktop.

  • The Fix: Press Alt + Z to open the overlay. Click the Gear icon (Settings). Scroll down to Privacy Control. Toggle "Desktop Capture" to ON. This forces ShadowPlay to record everything on your primary monitor, regardless of whether it recognizes the game.

2. Restart the NVIDIA Local System Container Service

If you press your record hotkey and absolutely nothing happens (not even a red line through the recording icon), the background service has crashed.

  • The Fix: Press the Windows Key, type Services.msc, and hit Enter. Scroll down the list until you find NVIDIA Local System Container. Right-click it and select Restart. Do the same for NVIDIA Display Container LS. This forces the overlay to reboot without restarting your PC.

3. ShadowPlay Not Recording Audio (Or Discord Audio)

You saved the clip, but it's completely silent, or your mic is missing. This happens when NVIDIA splits your audio tracks into two separate files, and your media player (like VLC) is only playing track one.

  • The Fix: Press Alt + Z > Settings > Audio. Under the microphone settings, change "Separate both tracks" to "Create a single track". This bakes your microphone, Discord, and game audio into one single audio layer that works flawlessly in all video players and editors.

4. You Ran Out of Dedicated Temp Space

ShadowPlay requires "Temporary File" space to constantly write the last 5 minutes of gameplay. If your C: drive is full, Instant Replay will immediately turn itself off.

  • The Fix: Go to Settings > Recordings. Change the "Temporary Files" directory to a drive that has at least 20GB of free space.

Why ShadowPlay's Built-in Notifications Are Broken

Even if you apply all the fixes above, you are going to face a harsh reality: NVIDIA updates break ShadowPlay constantly.

One day it works perfectly, and the next day—after a silent driver update—Instant Replay turns itself off. Because NVIDIA's overlay notifications only pop up if the software is working, you get absolutely no warning when the software has crashed in the background.

You play for three hours, hit Alt + F10 to save an insane clutch, and realize the icon in the corner was missing the entire time. The clip is gone forever.
