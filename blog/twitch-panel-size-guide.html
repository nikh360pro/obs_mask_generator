Twitch Panel Size Guide 2026: Best Dimensions & Formats

By Nikhil Agarwal • January 20, 2026 • 4 min read

Twitch panels are the backbone of your channel page. They direct viewers to your donation page, Discord server, and social media. But if your panels are the wrong size, they will look blurry, stretched, or unprofessional.

📏 The Golden Numbers for 2026

  • Width: Exactly 320 pixels (Required)
  • Height: Flexible (Recommended 75px - 150px)
  • Max File Size: 2.9 MB per panel
  • Format: PNG (Best quality) or GIF (Animation)

Why 320px Width Matters

Twitch's interface is hard-coded to display panels at 320 pixels wide. If you design a panel that is 600px wide, Twitch will automatically shrink it down to 320px. This resizing process often makes text look blurry or "crunchy".

Pro Tip: Always design your canvas at exactly 320px width to ensure what you see is what you get.

Height Best Practices

While width is fixed, height is flexible. However, consistency is key:

  • Standard Headers: 320px × 100px (Most popular)
  • Tall Info Cards: 320px × 300px (Great for "Rules" or "PC Specs")
  • Small Buttons: 320px × 50px (Good for social links)

File Formats: PNG vs. JPG vs. GIF

Format Best For Pros Cons
PNG Most Panels Supports transparency, sharp text, no compression artifacts. Larger file size than JPG.
GIF Animated Panels Allows movement (glow, sparkle, bounce). Limited to 256 colors, creating "banding" on gradients.
JPG Photos Smallest file size. No transparency (white corners), compression artifacts on text.

Verdict: Always use PNG unless you specifically need animation. Never use JPG for graphics with text.

Troubleshooting Common Issues

1. "My transparent corners have a white background"

This happens if you save your file as a JPG. JPGs do not support alpha channels (transparency). Re-save your file as a PNG-24.

2. "My text looks blurry on Twitch"

This is usually caused by uploading a large image (e.g., 1920px wide) and letting Twitch resize it. Resize your image to 320px width on your computer before uploading.

3. "File size too large" error

Twitch has a strict 2.9 MB limit per panel. This is common with animated GIFs. To fix this:

  • Reduce the number of frames in your GIF
  • Reduce the height of the panel
  • Use a simpler color palette

Panel Design Best Practices

Getting the dimensions right is just the first step. Here are the design principles that make the top streamers' panels stand out from the crowd:

  • Use a consistent color palette: Your panels, banner, and overlay should all use the same 2–3 colors. Inconsistency makes even good art look amateur.
  • Keep text minimal and large: Panel text is small on Twitch. Use fewer words at a larger font size. "Discord Server" is better than "Click here to join our Discord community!"
  • Use icons alongside text: A Discord logo + "Discord" reads faster than the word alone. Users scan, they don't read.
  • Match your personal brand: Your panels are part of your channel's identity. A horror game streamer and a variety casual streamer should have very different panel aesthetics.
  • Always include a clickable link: An About Me panel with no link is a missed opportunity. Every panel should either have a link or serve as visual decoration.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many panels can I have on Twitch?

Twitch allows up to 20 panels on your channel page. Most successful streamers use 6–12 panels. More than 12 starts to feel cluttered. Prioritize the panels viewers actually interact with: Donate, Discord, Schedule, and Social Media.

Should I use image-only panels or image + text panels?

Both work, but image-only panels with text baked into the graphic are more visually consistent. Twitch's text box below each panel uses a small, plain font that rarely matches your panel design. Most professional streamers embed all text directly into the panel image and leave the text box empty or minimal.

Can I use animated panels on Twitch?

Yes! Twitch supports animated GIF panels. An animated panel (like a glowing border or looping icon) can make your channel feel premium and dynamic. Keep the animation subtle — flashing or rapidly changing panels are distracting and can cause issues for viewers with photosensitivity.

Why do my panels look blurry on Twitch?

This usually means you uploaded the panel at too low a resolution and Twitch stretched it. Always create your panels at exactly 320 × 160 px (or a multiple like 640 × 320 px) and export at 72–96 DPI. Uploading a 100px wide image that Twitch then scales to 320px will always look blurry.
