How to Remove Your Video Background Online

This free tool uses AI segmentation to detect you in your webcam feed and make your background fully transparent — all in real time, entirely in your browser.

  1. Click Start Camera and allow camera access when prompted.
  2. Your background will be removed within a few seconds.
  3. The checkerboard pattern in the preview means the background is transparent.
  4. Adjust Edge Smoothing to improve the quality of your cut-out edges.
  5. For OBS: add this page as a Browser Source with Allow Transparency enabled.

What Is the Output Format?

The output canvas has a fully transparent background — only you are visible. When used as an OBS Browser Source with Allow Transparency enabled, OBS composites you directly onto your stream scene with no background. This is how professional streamers achieve the no-green-screen look.

How Is This Different From remove.bg?

remove.bg processes still images uploaded to their servers. This tool processes your live webcam video entirely in your browser in real time at 15-30 FPS. No image leaves your device. It is specifically designed for live video streaming, video calls, and OBS integration.
